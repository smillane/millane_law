About John Millane

John Millane has over 30 years of legal experience, including trial and advisory work. His practice serves clients in Buffalo and the surrounding communities, with a focus on estate planning and wills. He also handles real estate, traffic matters, family law and divorce, and criminal defense.

John holds a law degree from the University at Buffalo School of Law and an MBA from Golden Gate University. He is also a retired U.S. Marine Corps Major, having served for 26 years in the infantry and as a judge advocate.
